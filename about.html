how i got here

i graduated from the university of south carolina and moved to nashville to play music. honky-tonks, music row, studio time. a friend of a friend connected me to vaco, i started their national recruiting practice, and over time i narrowed in on tech.

15+ years later i still think recruiting is the best seat in the house. you see how hiring really works: who gets the call back, why referrals get read first, and what happens in the debrief after you hang up. i'm a professional friend maker, and most of what i teach comes from that seat.

along the way i started building communities. one dallas meetup went from a 5-person lunch to 225 rsvps. i've thrown 100+ events across the globe, and today i lead community and marketing at torc (randstad digital): meetups, conference booths, creators and the online community. the full story is here.

guidance counselor 2.0

we had guidance counselors in school. then we got a career and nobody. so in 2017 i started guidance counselor 2.0, a show for job seekers and hiring managers. 500+ episodes later it's live on youtube with recruiters, engineers and founders, from google devrel to cognition, plus solo series like the 8-week ai job search: what job you actually want, the hit list, resume, linkedin, dms, mock interviews and offers.

creator + ai marketing

i run my brand like an ai-first marketing team. i set the creative direction, and a crew of ai agents handles the publishing, clipping, outbound and reporting, so one person ships what used to take a team. every live becomes a 5-7 minute youtube edit, 3 subtitled shorts and a thumbnail, and it all goes out across youtube, x, linkedin, instagram, tiktok, threads, bluesky and substack.
